# Brag Plan: Gurbani Wallpapers

## What is this app?
A free Android app where you find any line of Gurbani and turn it into a calm phone wallpaper or status image; the Gurbani comes live from BaniDB and can never be edited.

## The angle
Quiet and respectful, not a joke. The claim that matters most to this audience is trust: the sacred text is never retyped or edited, it is shown exactly as received. The video shows the working flow (search, choose, result) and ends on that promise.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
"Find a line of Gurbani. Turn it into a wallpaper." Plain, calm, on the app's deep navy with gold.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The real search screen: "First letters" and "Random shabad" (a tap ring on Random shabad).
- Four real finished wallpapers arriving one by one in different calm backgrounds (Kesri, Neela, Sarovar, Gold leaf).
- The trust line: "Straight from BaniDB. Shown exactly as it is received. Never edited."

## Outro / punchline
App icon, "Gurbani Wallpapers", "Free on Google Play".

## User flow worth showing
Search by first letters -> pick a calm background -> finished wallpaper.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: quiet, respectful product film
- Interpretation: slow soft crossfades, long holds, no jokes, no hype words, no music or sound effects.

## Format: vertical, 1080x1920
## Duration: about 23.6 seconds (follows the narration)

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #0E1B2C (deep navy), card #15263B
- Accent: #C9A24A / #E0B85C (gold)
- Text: #EEF0F2, muted #9FB0C2
- Display font: Outfit; body font: Inter (closest bundled fonts to the app's UI font)
- Strongest visual element: the real wallpapers made by the app, shown as real screenshots

## Respect rules for this video
- No Gurbani is typed, animated or read aloud by the video. Gurbani appears only inside real app screenshots and wallpapers exactly as the app exported them.
- No pictures of people. No jokes. No music or sound effects.

## Share copy (draft)
Gurbani Wallpapers is live on Google Play: find any line of Gurbani and turn it into a calm wallpaper. Gurbani comes live from BaniDB, shown exactly as it is received.

## Audio direction
- Role: intentional silence apart from a calm narration (Kokoro voice af_heart)
- Music: none (respect for the content); SFX: none
- Music cue guidance: not applicable (no music)
- Audio-reactive treatment: none
- Restraint rule: nothing may compete with the narration
