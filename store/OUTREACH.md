# Outreach to Sikh pages and creators: Gurbani Wallpapers

Goal: get 5 to 10 existing Sikh pages (strangers with an audience) to try the
app and, if they like it, share it. One honest post from the right page can
bring hundreds of installs.

Link to send: https://sardarjidevelopers.com/gurbani-wallpapers/
(has the Play button, screenshots and the privacy policy).
Play link: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.gurbaniwallpapers.gurbani_wallpapers

## Rules (these protect your accounts)

- Send each message yourself, by hand. No bots, no auto-DM tools, no copy-paste
  blasts. Instagram and Facebook limit and ban accounts that do that.
- Send at most 10 messages a day, and write the first line for that page.
- Free offer only. You are not paying for posts. If asked for money, say no (reply 3).
- Never ask anyone to tap ads, and never offer anything for a review.
- If a page does post about the app and was paid or given something, they must
  mark it as a paid partnership. You are giving nothing, so nothing to mark.
- Respect "no": one follow-up after 5 days, then stop.

## Step 1: find 20 pages (about 30 minutes)

Good pages post Gurbani, kirtan clips, Gurpurab greetings, Sikh history or
wallpapers, and their followers are mostly Sikh and Punjabi speakers.

Where to look:
- Instagram search: "gurbani", "sikh quotes", "waheguru", "gurbani status",
  "punjabi quotes", "gurmukhi". Open the Reels and Accounts tabs.
- Open 3 accounts you like, then tap the "suggested accounts" arrow on their
  profile: Instagram shows similar pages.
- YouTube search: "gurbani status", "shabad whatsapp status", then look at
  channels under 100k subscribers.
- Facebook: Sikh and Gurbani pages that post daily.

Pick pages that are:
- Active (posted in the last 7 days), 5k to 100k followers. Very big pages
  rarely answer; very tiny ones have no reach.
- Respectful of Gurbani (no jokes, no edits of Gurbani text).
- Taking DMs (a Message button, or a contact email in the bio).

Skip pages that: sell followers, post inappropriate content, or edit or
retype Gurbani text carelessly.

Put each one in the tracker (`outreach-tracker.csv`, opens in Excel or Google Sheets).

## Step 2: the messages

Replace [Name], [page], and the personal line. Keep it short.

### Message 1: first DM

> Waheguru Ji Ka Khalsa, Waheguru Ji Ki Fateh 🙏
> [One real line about their page, e.g. "I love the daily shabad posts on [page]."]
>
> I made a small free Android app called Gurbani Wallpapers. You search any
> line of Gurbani, pick a calm background, and save it as a wallpaper or
> WhatsApp status. The Gurbani comes live from BaniDB and can't be edited, and
> it has no pictures of the Gurus.
>
> Would you like to try it? No payment, no strings. If you like it and want to
> share it with your followers, I'd be grateful, and if you have ideas to
> improve it I'd love to hear them. 🙏
> https://sardarjidevelopers.com/gurbani-wallpapers/

### Message 2: follow-up, once, after 5 days with no reply

> Waheguru Ji 🙏 just a gentle reminder in case my message got buried. No
> pressure at all. If you ever want to try the app, the link is
> https://sardarjidevelopers.com/gurbani-wallpapers/. Thank you for the
> seva you do on [page].

### Message 3: if they ask "how much?"

> Thank you for asking. I'm a small independent developer and I'm not able to
> pay for posts right now. If you like the app, a share would help a lot. I'm
> also happy to make a wallpaper in a style your followers would like. 🙏

### Message 4: if they say yes

> Thank you so much! 🙏 Here's everything in one place:
> - App page with screenshots: https://sardarjidevelopers.com/gurbani-wallpapers/
> - Play Store: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.gurbaniwallpapers.gurbani_wallpapers
> One line you can use: "Make a wallpaper or status from any line of Gurbani
> with the free Gurbani Wallpapers app. Gurbani comes live from BaniDB."
> Please tell me if anything doesn't work and I'll fix it quickly.

### Email version (for pages with a contact email)

Subject: A free Gurbani wallpaper app for your followers

Use Message 1 in the body and sign with your name, "Sardar Ji Developers" and
support@sardarjidevelopers.com.

## Step 3: after they reply

- Say thank you and mark it in the tracker.
- If they post, watch Play Console Statistics for the installs that day.
- Reply to every comment you can on their post (as the developer).
- Send a thank-you after a week and share their post to your Pinterest/Instagram.

## What to expect

Most pages will not reply. Plan on 1 reply for every 10 to 15 messages. That
is normal. 20 careful messages are better than 100 rushed ones.
