# Pinterest plan: Gurbani Wallpapers

Landing page for every pin to link to (has the Play button and screenshots):
https://sardarjidevelopers.github.io/gurbani-wallpapers/

Why Pinterest: people search it for phone wallpapers, and a pin keeps showing
in search for months, so every pin is a small free ad that doesn't expire.

Rules: all Gurbani on a pin must come from the app itself (live from BaniDB,
shown exactly as published). Never retype Gurbani into another design tool.
No pictures of the Gurus on pins either.

## Step 1: set up (15 minutes, once)

1. Sign up at pinterest.com/business with a NEW business account for the
   brand, not your personal profile:
   - Business name: Sardar Ji Developers (or Gurbani Wallpapers)
   - Username: sardarjidevelopers (or gurbaniwallpapers)
   - Website: https://sardarjidevelopers.github.io
   - Country/language: India, English
2. Profile photo: `store/play-icon-512.png`.
3. Bio (under 160 characters):
   `Free Gurbani wallpapers and status images for Android. Made with respect, Gurbani from BaniDB.`
4. Claim your website: Settings > Claimed accounts > Claim > Website. Choose
   "Add HTML tag", copy the `<meta name="p:domain_verify" ...>` line and send
   it to Claude. It goes into the website, then press Verify.

## Step 4: keep it going

- Post 3 to 5 pins a week (one new wallpaper each time) for the first 2 months.
  Pinterest rewards steady posting, not bursts.
- Re-pin each new pin to a second relevant board a few days later.
- Around each Gurpurab or festival, post 5 festival pins 2 weeks BEFORE the date,
  because Pinterest users search ahead.
- Pinterest Analytics (Business): note which pins get saves and clicks, and
  make more like them.
- Add a Pinterest "Save" link to your WhatsApp and Reels posts later.

## Don't

- Don't buy followers, use auto-posting bots, or post the same pin to many
  boards at once. Pinterest treats this as spam.
- Don't ask people to tap ads.
