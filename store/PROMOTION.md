# Gurbani Wallpapers: promotion kit (version 1)

Play link: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.gurbaniwallpapers.gurbani_wallpapers

Rules: ask only for honest reviews. Never offer anything in return for a
review, never ask anyone to tap ads, never buy installs.

## Week 1: first 20-30 reviews (family, friends, sangat)

Send one by one (not in a big group blast), with the link:

> Waheguru Ji Ka Khalsa, Waheguru Ji Ki Fateh 🙏
> I made a free app where you can search any line of Gurbani and turn it into a
> wallpaper or WhatsApp status. The Gurbani comes live from BaniDB and can't be
> edited. Please try it on your Android phone, and if you like it, an honest
> review on Play Store would really help. Thank you!
> <PLAY LINK>

Tip: after someone installs it, ask them to make one wallpaper. People who've
used it write better reviews.

## Week 1-2: WhatsApp groups (family, gurdwara, sangat)

1. Open the app, make a wallpaper from a line you love, tap Share.
2. Post it in the group with this message:

> Made this wallpaper with a free app 🙏 You can make your own from any line of
> Gurbani, free on Play Store: <PLAY LINK>

Post at most once a week in each group. Post again around Gurpurabs.

## Weeks 2-4: Reels and Shorts (15-20 seconds)

Screen-record on your phone:
1. Open the app, type the first letters of a line you know (2 sec).
2. Tap a result, pick a background and font (5 sec).
3. Save, then show it on your lock screen (5 sec).
4. End card: "Free on Play Store. Link in bio."

Caption:
> Turn any line of Gurbani into a wallpaper in 10 seconds 🙏 Free on Play Store (link in bio)
> #Gurbani #Waheguru #SikhWallpaper #GurbaniStatus #Gurmukhi #Sikh #Punjabi

Post 3 times a week. Use different lines and backgrounds each time.

## Facebook groups and Reddit

Read each group's rules first. Many allow a helpful free tool if you are
open that you made it. Use a short post:

> I made a free Android app that turns any line of Gurbani into a wallpaper or
> status image. Gurbani is loaded live from BaniDB, shown exactly as published.
> Feedback welcome: <PLAY LINK>

## Every week

- Play Console, Statistics: note which search words bring installs.
- Reply to every review, thank people and fix problems they mention.
- Ask the next 5 people you meet who use Android to try it.

## After 20+ reviews at 4 stars or more

Try a small Google Ads app campaign (INR 200-300 a day), aimed at India,
Canada, the UK and Australia. Stop it if each install costs more than the
app earns per user.
